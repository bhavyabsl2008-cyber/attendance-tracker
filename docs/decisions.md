# Architecture Decisions

---

## ADR-001

Decision

Documentation-first development.

Reason

Every AI should begin from the same source of truth.

---

## ADR-002

Decision

Business logic belongs in the domain layer.

Reason

UI should only display information.

---

## ADR-003

Decision

Runway Bar replaces circular progress indicators.

Reason

Attendance behaves like a trajectory, not completion.

---

## ADR-004

Decision

Documentation must evolve alongside implementation.

Reason

Documentation that falls behind becomes misleading.
