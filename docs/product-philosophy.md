# Product Philosophy

## Why Presynce Exists

Students don't struggle because they cannot calculate attendance.

They struggle because they don't know what their attendance actually means.

A percentage alone cannot answer questions like:

- Can I skip tomorrow?
- How many classes do I need to attend to recover?
- Which subject is at risk?
- What happens if I miss next week's lab?

Presynce exists to answer those questions before students have to ask them.

---

# Mission

Transform attendance from a passive record into an active planning tool.

---

# Vision

Presynce should become the operating system for academic attendance.

Not just a tracker.

Not just a calculator.

An intelligent assistant that continuously helps students make better attendance decisions.

---

# Product Principles

## Every number must explain itself.

Never display data without interpretation.

---

## Reduce anxiety.

The interface should make students feel informed, not judged.

---

## Simplicity wins.

Complex calculations happen behind the scenes.

The UI should feel effortless.

---

## Action over information.

Every screen should help users decide what to do next.

---

## Trust is everything.

Calculations must always be accurate.

Predictions must clearly communicate confidence.

---

# Success Metrics

A successful user should be able to answer within five seconds:

- Am I safe?
- Can I skip?
- What should I do next?

without needing to perform any mental calculations.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Become the default attendance platform used by students across universities.
